# Relief Boat

Request: How often did the relief boat reach a rock light in winter? (#research/run-2)

## Findings

- In winter the relief boat was often a week or more late, waiting for the swell to drop.

## Sources

- Board of Lights relief boat logs, 1952 to 1956.
