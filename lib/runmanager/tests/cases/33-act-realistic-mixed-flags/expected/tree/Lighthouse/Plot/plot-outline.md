# Plot Outline

## Act one

- Mara's routine on the rock. The first supply boat of autumn.
- The Board of Lights writes: a relief keeper is coming in November.
- Mara refuses to prepare the second bedroom.

## Act two

- The relief keeper arrives. Mara sets rules on the jetty.
- The fog signal fails on the third night. They share a watch.
- He finds the logbook. She finds his letters from home.
- The causeway floods, and the village cannot reach them.

## Act three

- The storm. Mara leaves the light for the first time in eleven years.
- The relief keeper keeps the lamp burning alone.
- Morning. The boat under the pier is gone.

## Open

- Does Mara stay, or does she take the relief keeper's place on the mainland?
