# Idea - Mara Lighthouse

- The Harrow Point light stands on a rock at the end of the headland.
- Mara lives alone in the keeper's cottage at the foot of the tower.
- The lamp is a fourth-order lens that has never been replaced. #status/provisional
%% Agent, reject the dog. It makes her too cosy. %%
- The tower has 94 steps. Mara counts them every morning.
- The fog signal sounds for three seconds every thirty, all night long.
- Mara times her supply runs around the tide table, because the causeway floods twice a month. #status/provisional

## Routine

- Dawn: Mara puts out the lamp, writes the weather in the logbook, and counts the boats.
- Dusk: she trims the wicks and lights the lamp. The ritual never changes.
- She has not spent a night off the rock in eleven years.
