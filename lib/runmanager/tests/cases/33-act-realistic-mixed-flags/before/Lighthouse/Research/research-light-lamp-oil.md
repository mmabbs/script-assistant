# Lamp Oil

Request: How was lamp oil delivered to remote lights? (#research/run-1)

## Findings

- Remote lights were resupplied by a tender, usually every six to eight weeks.
- Oil came in sealed drums and was stored in a separate stone oil house, away from the cottage.
- Keepers logged every litre burned, and the Board audited the logbook each year.
- A missed delivery meant dimming the lamp, which was a reportable offence.

## Useful for the story

- The oil house gives the rock a second small building and a place to hide things.
- The audit makes the logbook matter: Mara cannot lie in it, even when she wants to.

## Sources

- Board of Lights keeper's handbook, 1951 edition.
