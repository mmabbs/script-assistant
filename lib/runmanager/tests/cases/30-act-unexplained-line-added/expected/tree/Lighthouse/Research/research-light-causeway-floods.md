# Causeway Floods

Request: How often did the causeway flood in the 1950s? (#research/run-2)

## Findings

- Spring tides covered the causeway about twice a month.
- In an easterly gale it could stay under for a full day.

## Sources

- Harbour office notice book, 1949 to 1961.
