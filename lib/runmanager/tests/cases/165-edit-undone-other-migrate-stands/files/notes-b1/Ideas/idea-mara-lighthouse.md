# Idea - Mara Lighthouse

- Mara lives alone in the keeper's cottage at the foot of the tower.

## Routine

- She has not spent a night off the rock in eleven years.
- Mara has never once left the light during a storm. #status/provisional
- The supply boat captain calls her "Miss Quill" and nobody else does. #status/provisional
