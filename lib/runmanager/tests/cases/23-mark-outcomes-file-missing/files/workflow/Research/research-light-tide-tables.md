# Tide Tables

Request: How did printed tide tables reach small villages in the 1950s? (#research/run-2)

## Findings

- Small villages got their tide tables from a printed almanac sold at the chandler's, a new one each January.
- The harbour office copied the week's tides onto a slate beside the pier.

## Sources

- Carrick Bay chandler's price list, 1954.
