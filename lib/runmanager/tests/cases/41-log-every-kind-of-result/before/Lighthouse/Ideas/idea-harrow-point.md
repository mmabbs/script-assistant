# Idea - Harrow Point

- Harrow Point is a fishing village of about two hundred people.
- The pub, the chapel and the co-op share one wall. There is no other street.
- Every kitchen window in the village has a view of the light.

## Winters

- The village empties out in January. The young people go to the mainland for work and come back for Easter.
- The co-op sells diesel, bread and batteries, and nothing else.
- The chapel stays heated all winter. It is the warmest room in the village.
