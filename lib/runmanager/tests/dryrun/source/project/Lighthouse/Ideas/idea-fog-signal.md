# Idea - Fog Signal

- The fog signal is a compressed-air horn: one blast of three seconds every thirty.
- On thick nights it runs from dusk to dawn.
- The sound carries nine miles over open water, and about four feet into the cottage walls.
- Mara stopped hearing it years ago. The relief keeper hears nothing else for a week.

## Scenes

- Night one: the relief keeper sits up in bed, counting the blasts. Mara is asleep across the yard.
- The morning the fog signal fails. The silence is worse than the noise.
