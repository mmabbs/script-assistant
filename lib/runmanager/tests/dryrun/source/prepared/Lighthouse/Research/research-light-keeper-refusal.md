# Keeper Refusal

Request: Could a lone keeper legally refuse a relief posting? (#research/run-2)

## Findings

- No source states whether a keeper could refuse a posting outright.
- The staffing circular says keepers "will proceed to the station named" and says nothing about refusing.

## Useful for the story

- Nothing firm. Mara can believe she may refuse and be wrong, but this note does not support either answer.

## Open questions

- Whether refusal counted as resignation. No source says.

## Sources

- Board of Lights staffing circular, 1953.
- Keeper's handbook, 1951 edition.
