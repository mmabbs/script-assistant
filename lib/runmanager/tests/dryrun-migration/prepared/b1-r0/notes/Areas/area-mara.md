# Mara

- Mara Quill is the keeper of the Harrow Point light.
- Mara's father kept the light before her.
- Mara repaints the lamp room every spring.
- Mara is afraid of deep water, though she has lived beside it all her life.
- She answers the radio on the first ring and says as little as possible.
- Mara has not left the headland for a night in eleven years.
- Mara is wary of the mainland doctor. #status/provisional
- Mara keeps a logbook and has never let anyone read it.

## Rationale

- I want her alone out there, with nobody to explain herself to.
- Her father keeping the light first gives her a reason to stay that isn't only duty.
