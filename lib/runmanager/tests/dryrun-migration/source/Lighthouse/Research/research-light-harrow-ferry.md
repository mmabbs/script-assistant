# Harrow Ferry

Request: How did the ferry to Harrow Point run in the 1950s? (#research/run-1)

## Findings

- The ferry ran twice a day, morning and late afternoon, until the service was cut in 1962.
- The crossing to Carrick Bay took 35 minutes in fair weather and was often cancelled in November.
- Summer crews were local boys, hired for the season at fourteen or fifteen.
- The ferry carried the mail, so the village post was always a day late after a cancellation.
- Winter cancellations were announced by a flag at the end of the pier.

## Useful for the story

- A summer job on the ferry fits Tobias: young, local, and always on the water.
- The flag on the pier gives Mara a way to know the boat isn't coming.
