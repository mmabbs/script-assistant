# r1 findings

- Relief keepers were engaged for a season at a time, usually six months.
- The contract named a lodging allowance, a coal ration, and a clause on leaving the station unattended.
- A relief keeper reported to the principal keeper, not to the Board.
- Source: Board of Lights staffing circular, 1953.
