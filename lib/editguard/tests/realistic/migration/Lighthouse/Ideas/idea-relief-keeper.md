# Idea - Relief Keeper

- The relief keeper arrives in November, when the Board of Lights rotates its staff.
- He is young, maybe twenty-two, and has been posted to a light he has never seen.
- Mara asked for no one. The Board sent him anyway.
- The relief keeper has never worked a hand-wound lamp.
    - [?] Who teaches him, Mara or the logbook?
- The relief keeper could be someone from the mainland who has never seen the sea and is frightened of the water.

## First meeting

- Mara watches the supply boat through the telescope for an hour before she walks down.
- She tells him the rules on the jetty, before he has put his bag down.
- He asks where the toilet is. It is the first thing anyone has asked her in a month.
