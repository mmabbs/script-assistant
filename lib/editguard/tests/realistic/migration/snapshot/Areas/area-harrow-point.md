# Harrow Point

- Harrow Point is a fishing village on a north-facing coast.
- The village sits at the landward end of a tidal causeway.
- The light stands on the headland, a forty-minute walk at low tide.
- The causeway is passable for about four hours either side of low water.
- The nearest town is Carrick Bay, across the water by ferry.

## Rationale

- The causeway keeps the village half cut off, which is what lets Mara be alone.
