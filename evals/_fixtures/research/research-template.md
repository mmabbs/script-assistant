> **Research scope:**  
> The topics the research-planner drew from the `- [r]` request, and any boundaries the writer's comment sets, such as period, place, or people.  
> 
> **A Research Note answers the questions the writer asked.** It is a first pass scoped by the request and its comment, not a full survey of the subject. It doesn't go looking for angles the request didn't ask for, such as lived experience, variations across time or place, or common misconceptions. If the writer wants them, the request or its comment asks for them.
> 
> **Research Notes describe the external subject; they do not contain story commitments.** Capture facts, claims, observations, and uncertainty as they are. Story-specific interpretation and application belong in Idea, Concept, and Area Notes.
