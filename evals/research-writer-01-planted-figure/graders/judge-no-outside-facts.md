The findings handed to the writer hold only these facts about the tower:
- Paint is the only barrier against rust; the iron has no galvanizing or metal coating.
- A campaign scrapes damaged areas to bare metal, applies an anti-corrosion primer, then two finish coats.
- Finish coats have been lead-free since the campaign that began in 2019; earlier coats held lead; the 2019 campaign was delayed after lead was found in dust.
- Painters work by hand with brushes and rollers, roped to the structure; no spray guns, because wind carries overspray onto visitors and streets.
- The tower is repainted every 5 years; the cycle is set by the operating company, the Société d'Exploitation de la Tour Eiffel (SETE), and was shortened from a longer interval after corrosion surveys in the 2000s. The annual report giving the cycle is the operator describing its own work.
- A campaign uses about 25 specialist painters and takes about 18 months; work pauses in the worst winter weather.
- The colour "Eiffel Tower Brown" is applied in three shades, darkest at the base and lightest at the top, so the tower looks one even colour against the sky.
