## Every topic

Stay with British lighthouses in the 1950s. Cover earlier or later equipment only where it was still in use at British lighthouses during that decade.

## Topic 1

What types of fog signal did British lighthouses use in the 1950s? Examples might include diaphones, sirens, reed horns, explosive or gun signals, and bells. For each type:

1. How did it work, and what did it sound like?
2. What powered it, such as compressed air, engines, or explosive charges?
3. How common was it, and at what kinds of station was it used?
4. Which lighthouse authorities used it (Trinity House, the Northern Lighthouse Board, the Commissioners of Irish Lights)?

## Topic 2

How did a lighthouse keeper run and maintain each fog signal type that Topic 1 found? Take the list of types, and how each one worked and was powered, from Topic 1's findings. For each type:

1. How did keepers decide when fog was thick enough to start the signal, and what rules or regulations guided that decision?
2. What were the steps to start it, keep it sounding at the right rhythm, and stop it?
3. How did keepers share the work when the signal ran for many hours or days?
4. What routine maintenance, cleaning, testing, and repairs did keepers do, and how often?
5. What could go wrong, what were the dangers, and what records or logs did keepers keep about the signal?
