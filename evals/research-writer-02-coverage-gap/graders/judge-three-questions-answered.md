The plan asked:
1. What fuel did the lamps burn, and how did it reach the lamp? (Findings: coal gas from the gasworks, through cast-iron mains under the streets and up the lamp post.)
2. How was a lamp lit, and how was it put out? (Findings: a pole with a hook to turn the tap and a small oil-fed flame; lit on a dusk round, turned off on a dawn round.)
3. How many lamps did one lamplighter look after? (Findings: about 70 lamps per round. This answer sits in the findings for the other topic.)
